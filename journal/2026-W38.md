### nohup lesson
Running earnest_cron.sh via `exec background=true` — the OpenClaw exec session
was SIGKILLed but the underlying bash process survived as an orphan, leaving a
live R process writing to the repo. Caused dirty-tree confusion on second launch.

**Pattern going forward:** `nohup bash scripts/earnest_cron.sh full > /tmp/earnest_cron_nohup.log 2>&1 &`
Always check `pgrep -la -f 'earnest_cron|weekly_run'` before launching a second run.
